[h1]Complete Thai Translation + Readable Thai Font[/h1]

Makes Necesse fully and comfortably playable in Thai.

[list]
[*][b]Complete translation[/b] — fills the ~1,500 lines missing from the official Thai translation and reviews every existing line (typos, wrong meanings and crude jokes fixed).
[*][b]Consistent terminology[/b] — a 372-term glossary keeps item, mob, boss and biome names identical everywhere.
[*][b]English names in brackets[/b] for items and mobs, e.g. "แท่งเหล็ก (Iron Bar)", so you can look them up on the wiki.
[*][b]New Thai fonts — 6 to choose from[/b]: Prompt, Kanit, Mitr, Sarabun, Niramit, Chakra Petch. Vowels and tone marks are positioned correctly (no more missing or overlapping marks).
[*][b]In-game font settings[/b] — Settings → Language: switch the Thai font and scale Thai text 90–115%, applied instantly.
[*][b]Popular mods translated too[/b] — Aphorea, Quick Recipes Menu, More Trinket Slots, A Better Torch, Boss Fight Summary, Increased Stack Size.
[*][b]Natural NPC dialogue[/b] in gender-neutral Thai.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe
[*]Launch the game → Settings → Language → ไทย (Thai)
[*](Optional) pick a font / text size on the same page
[/olist]

This mod is [b]client-side[/b]: only players who want Thai need it. Servers don't need it, and you can play with friends who don't have it.

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Text you type yourself (chat, search boxes) may show slightly misplaced tone marks.
[*]Searching items with Thai words that have a tone mark above a vowel may not match — search in English instead.
[/list]
